# Cadence Templates by Persona

Templates are organized by **persona** rather than vertical, because the same persona converts on similar message structures across industries. Adapt the **proof point** and the **specific signal** to your vertical and product — those come from `config/settings.md`.

---

## VP Operations / Operational Leader

**Cold Email Template:**
Subject: [Specific operational metric] at [Company]

[Company] has been [specific recent signal — expansion, earnings mention, leadership change]. For operational leaders running [scale indicator from their public profile], [the operational tension your solution addresses] is usually one of the hardest levers to move.

We work with [comparable customer descriptor from settings] to [specific outcome with conservative number]. Worth 15 minutes to see if it applies to your situation?

## What customizes each template

The bracketed fields above pull from `config/settings.md`:

- `COMPANY_NAME` and `PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION` for your positioning line
- `KEY_OUTCOMES` for the proof points (use the conservative end of your range)
- `CUSTOMER_REFERENCES` for comparable customer descriptors (only ones with `approved_for_external: false` get the neutral descriptor; named references get the company name)
- `KEY_PAIN_POINTS` per vertical for the buyer's-language pain point

The cadence skill reads these and substitutes them automatically — but the structure above is what makes each message convert.
