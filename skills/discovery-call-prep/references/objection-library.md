# Objection Library

## Budget & Timing Objections

**"We don't have budget for this right now."**
Response: "Understood — timing matters. Can I ask: is this a priority that's on the roadmap for later in the year, or is it not on the list at all? That'll help me understand whether it's worth staying in touch or whether we should revisit next cycle."
Follow-up: "If the ROI case were clear and fast-to-capture, would budget be findable?"

**"Our budget cycle doesn't start until [Q/month]."**
Response: "That's actually useful — that gives us time to build a solid case so you're ready to move when the window opens. What would need to be true for this to make the list?"
Follow-up: "Who else would need to see the case before it goes into the budget request?"

## Competitor / Incumbent Objections

**"We already work with [competitor]."**
Response: "That's fair — they do good work in some areas. I'm not here to replace everything they do. What I'd want to understand is whether there are gaps in what they cover — specifically around [specific capability]. Is that something that comes up?"
Follow-up: "Are you fully satisfied with how that piece works today?"

**"We built something internally."**
Response: "A lot of our best customers had internal solutions before we worked together. The question isn't usually whether it works — it's whether it scales and whether it's a core competency you want to keep investing in. Where are the edges of what your internal tool handles well?"

## "Send Me Info" / Stall Objections

**"Just send me something to review."**
Response: "I can do that — I just want to make sure I send you the right thing. Can I ask one quick question so I don't waste your time with something generic: [most relevant diagnostic question]?"
Follow-up: Direct to a specific asset based on their answer.

**"We're not looking at anything right now."**
Response: "That's fine. I'm not trying to rush a process. I'd rather ask: on a scale of 1–10, how much of a pain is [core pain point] for your team right now?"
Follow-up: If 6+: "What's keeping you from moving on it?" If <6: "Got it — when would be a better time to reconnect?"

## Technical / Risk Objections

**"How does this integrate with our [existing core systems]?"**
Response: "That's the right question to ask early. We have integrations with [relevant systems from settings]. The ones we don't have native connectors for, we've handled through API. What's your current stack — I want to give you an accurate answer."
Follow-up: Get the specific system and version; loop in technical resources if needed.

**"We've had bad experiences with implementations before."**
Response: "I hear that a lot — and it's a legitimate concern. What went wrong? I want to understand whether this is a category risk or a vendor-specific issue, because our implementation model is different in some specific ways."
Follow-up: Map their concern to your differentiation on deployment model.

## Champion / Stakeholder Objections

**"I'd need to bring in [other person] before we can go any further."**
Response: "Absolutely — I'd expect that. Who are the key people we'd need aligned? I'd love to understand the full decision team so we can make sure we're covering the right concerns for each person."
Follow-up: "What's the best way to get time with that person — would you make an intro, or should I reach out directly?"
